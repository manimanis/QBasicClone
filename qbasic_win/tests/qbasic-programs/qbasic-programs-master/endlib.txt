The Story of Xyzzilaus
   by Alice

A long time ago, in a place called Atlantis, there lived a(n) wizard who was called Xyzzilaus.
(His full name was Xyzzilaus the brave.)
He was only a poor, old knight. One day at his job, Xyzzilaus said,'Wow! I don't want this job anymore!'
'It pays only $50 a week, and that's not enough! I quit!'

Xyzzilaus started looking for a new job. When he wasn't actively searching, he was slacking at home, eating sour pizza wearing nothing but his hat.
Then he finally found a job! He became a(n) coder!
For the first few days, he really loved his new, shiny job.
Then his boss locked him in the basement.

Xyzzilaus stayed in the basement for many months.
Then he figured out how to escape from his dark prison.
He would take a(n) rock and throw it at the window.
Then he'd climb up, open the window, and jump out!
He would finally be able to go back to his castle!

However, the building was 10 stories high, so when Xyzzilaus jumped out the window, he fell to the ground and broke his leg.

THE END
